## Interview 1 — Passenger
**Would you use Ride4Free?**
Yeah, honestly I think so. I'm a student, I don't have a car, and public transport here is kind of a nightmare after 10pm. If I could just open an app and find someone going my way, that'd be huge.
**How would you use it?**
Probably for my commute to uni a few times a week, and maybe weekends when I'm heading somewhere further out. I'd look for rides in advance rather than last minute I think, just to feel more in control of my schedule.
**What would make you hesitant?**
Honestly? Getting in a stranger's car. Like, what do I actually know about this person? I'd need to see reviews, maybe a verified ID badge or something. If someone has zero ratings I'm probably not getting in. Also I'd want to know the ride is confirmed. I don't want to be standing outside waiting and have someone cancel on me at the last second.
**Anything else?**
Just keep it simple. I don't want to be fiddling with a complicated app when I'm half asleep in the morning.

## Interview 2 — Driver
**Would you use Ride4Free?**
Maybe. It depends on what I actually get out of it. I drive to work every day and the car is half empty, so in theory it makes sense. But I'm not going out of my way for someone, that's a hard line for me.
**How would you use it?**
Only for routes I'm already doing. My commute, maybe a trip to the supermarket, that kind of thing. I'd set my route and let the app find someone who fits, not the other way around.
**What would make you hesitant?**
A few things. First, having a stranger in my car makes me a bit nervous. What if they're weird, or leave a mess, or worse? I'd need to trust the vetting process completely. Second, if the detour to pick someone up adds more than like five minutes, I'm out. And if I'm burning my own fuel, I want to feel like I'm at least breaking even. The point system sounds interesting but I'd want to understand what those points are actually worth. Cosmetic profile stuff alone won't cut it for me.
**Anything else?**
I'd want full control. I pick who rides with me, I can see their profile before I accept, and I can cancel without hassle if something feels off.
